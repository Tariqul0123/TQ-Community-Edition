# AI Workforce

Providers and models are replaceable workers assigned to roles such as General, Research, Coding, Finance, Communications, Marketing, Data, and Background.

TQ remains responsible for policy, workflows, memory, and business state. Provider changes must not change a department's responsibility or bypass approvals.

Routing should consider task risk, quality, latency, quota, and availability. Use Local AI first for routine extraction, classification, summarization, indexing, and fallback work only after benchmarking.

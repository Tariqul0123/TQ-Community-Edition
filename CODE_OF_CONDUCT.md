# Code of Conduct

The TQ Community Edition community is committed to a respectful, safe, and constructive environment. Participants are expected to communicate in good faith, treat others with consideration, critique ideas rather than people, and respect differing viewpoints.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, retaliation against good-faith reporters, and unauthorized disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's private contact or vulnerability-reporting channel when available. Do not publish credentials, sensitive personal data, or exploit details in public issues. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This is an original TQ Community Edition policy and is not presented as an adaptation of the Contributor Covenant.
